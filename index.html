Log Spend Auditor

Find where your Splunk ingest budget is going — without sending your data anywhere.

Log Spend Auditor is a local-first desktop CLI for Splunk teams that analyzes aggregated ingest and usage metadata to identify high-cost datasets and optimization opportunities.

View product features

Ingest grows. Its value doesn't always keep up.

Splunk environments can accumulate large amounts of ingest from datasets whose operational value may no longer justify their cost.

Log Spend Auditor helps teams identify where investigation is worthwhile. It surfaces candidates for review, with the evidence behind them — it never tells you to delete data, and it does not promise savings.

How it works

  1. Connect or import

    Connect read-only to Splunk, or provide exported CSV metadata.

  2. Analyze

    Analyze ingest against usage and evidence signals.

  3. Review

    Review optimization candidates and the supporting evidence.

Works with real Splunk data through the REST API or CSV exports. Designed for Splunk teams.

Local-first by design

Read-only analysis that runs on your machine. No SaaS backend required.

Runs locally

Log Spend Auditor runs on the customer's machine.

No raw events

No raw Splunk events are sent to Log Spend Auditor servers. The analysis uses aggregated ingest and usage metadata.

No telemetry

The product collects no usage telemetry or analytics.

Your credentials stay with you

Splunk credentials are not uploaded to us.

Licensing only

Licensing uses Lemon Squeezy only for license activation and validation. No Splunk customer data is sent to Lemon Squeezy.

Read-only

It does not modify Splunk configuration or data.

Details are in the Privacy page.
