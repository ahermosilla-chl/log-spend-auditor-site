Free, no download

Three Splunk searches to see where your ingest goes.

Paste these into Splunk Search today. They need the search capability and read access to _internal and _audit — no admin role.

  1. 1. Your biggest datasets, in GB/day

    index=_internal source=*license_usage.log type="Usage" earliest=-30d@d latest=@d
| stats sum(b) as bytes by idx, st
| eval gb_per_day=round(bytes/1024/1024/1024/30, 2)
| sort - gb_per_day
| head 20
| rename idx as index, st as sourcetype

  2. 2. Which indexes people actually search

    index=_audit action=search info=granted search=* earliest=-30d
NOT user=splunk-system-user
| rex field=search max_match=10 "index\s*=\s*\"?(?<searched_index>[\w\-\*]+)"
| mvexpand searched_index
| stats count as searches dc(user) as users by searched_index
| sort - searches

  3. 3. Sourcetypes that went quiet

    | metadata type=sourcetypes index=*
| eval days_since_last_event=round((now()-recentTime)/86400, 1)
| table sourcetype, totalCount, days_since_last_event
| sort - days_since_last_event

Anything big in list 1 that never shows up in list 2 is worth a conversation. Matching them by hand gets slow past a few dozen datasets, and searches that reach data through macros, eventtypes, scheduled alerts or dashboards are easy to miss. That cross-check is what the free Quickscan automates.

FAQ

Questions Splunk admins ask first.

Do I have to give the tool my Splunk credentials?

No. In CSV mode you run the bundled searches yourself, export the results and point the tool at the folder. It never talks to Splunk. REST mode is optional and uses a read-only token you provide, which stays on your machine.

What Splunk permissions does it need?

The search capability plus read access to _internal (license usage) and _audit (search activity). No admin role. It never creates, modifies or deletes configuration or data.

Why does the free download go through a checkout?

Community is delivered through Lemon Squeezy so you get a permanent download link by email. It costs nothing and needs no license key.

I'm on a Mac or Windows laptop. Can I use it?

The current build runs on Linux x86_64 with glibc 2.35 or newer, for example a jump host, a VM or WSL2 with Ubuntu 22.04. Native macOS and Windows builds come later.

Will it tell me to delete data?

No. It classifies each index and sourcetype and shows the evidence behind it. Findings are candidates for review with the data owner; savings are an estimate, not a guarantee, and you decide what to change.

What do I get with Pro that Quickscan doesn't show?

The full inventory of every dataset, the evidence and explanation for each classification, the Data Value Score, recommendations, risks and methodology, as an HTML and Markdown report you can share. Here is a complete example.
